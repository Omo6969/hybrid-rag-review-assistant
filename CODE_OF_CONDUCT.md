# Code of Conduct

We value respectful, inclusive, and constructive collaboration. This Code of Conduct outlines expectations for participant behavior and steps for reporting unacceptable behavior.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful and courteous in all communications.
- Be considerate of different backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives.
- Give and receive constructive feedback focusing on code and ideas, not people.
- Avoid demeaning, discriminatory, or harassing language or actions.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sharing private information without consent.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers at the project email or to the course staff. Provide as much detail as possible (what happened, when, who was involved, and any evidence).

## Enforcement
Maintainers will review reports and may take actions including private warnings, temporary removal from the project, or informing course staff depending on severity.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces including GitHub issues, pull requests, email, chat, meetings, and in-person interactions related to this project.
